## What is Bubblescape?

A research tool that maps algorithmic filter bubbles on Dutch TikTok. Accounts are scraped, a hashtag co-occurrence graph is built, Louvain detects communities, and the structure is visualized as an interactive bubble map.

**Theoretical definition (keep this precise):**
> A bubble is a partially closed actual domain — a structured set of empirical exposures produced by the conjoint operation of algorithmic mechanisms, social structures, and individual agency. Bubbles are emergent properties, not purely psychological (confirmation bias) nor purely structural (algorithmic determinism). They persist because the structural mechanisms producing them are largely unobservable to the people inside them.

**Technically:** bubbles = clusters in a hashtag co-occurrence graph. No follow graph (following lists are private).

**Critical API findings:**
1. Responses are wrapped in `{"data": {...}}` — always unwrap
2. Video hashtags live in `"tag"` (not `"hashtags"`), as objects `{"name": "pvv", "entityId": "..."}`
3. Video `"content"` = plain string (caption)
4. Video ID = `"entityId"` (numeric), not `"id"` (URL)
5. Following lists are PRIVATE — 0 results. Do not use for the graph
6. Tag endpoint `/tiktok/tags/{tag}/videos` → 404 (broken)
7. Search endpoint `/tiktok/search/videos?query={tag}` → works
8. Pagination via `"nextCursor"`
9. 502/500 errors on fast requests → random 0.5–2s sleep in `_get()`
10. Audio metadata always available: `audio_id` + `audio_title`
11. **No subtitles/transcriptions available** — feature request submitted

## Feedback for the KonbiniAPI developer

*Based on research experience with 1,699 accounts and 47,192 videos on Dutch TikTok. Relevant for the API's product development.*

### Critical missing data fields

**1. Video language / language detection**
The biggest classification problem in our research: we cannot reliably say whether an account belongs to the Dutch information ecosystem without knowing the language of its videos. We currently run `langdetect` on captions as a proxy. A `language` field per video (or per account at the profile level) would make this trivial and save credits — right now we fully collect an account before we know whether it is relevant.

**2. Subtitles / transcriptions**
Already submitted as a feature request. Crucial for content analysis: a politician who speaks without hashtags is currently completely invisible in our graph. Many institutional accounts (public broadcasters, commercial broadcasters, party accounts) use no hashtags but produce rich spoken content. Without transcriptions we miss the substantive signal from precisely the accounts with the largest reach.

**3. Always return bio in the profile response**
From our data: ~35% of Dutch accounts have an informative bio. Bio text is the richest available signal for account classification — richer than hashtags, and available for accounts that use no hashtags. Needs verification: does `/tiktok/users/{username}` always return `bio`, even when empty? Or is the field sometimes omitted?

### Data-quality observation: hashtag poverty among institutional accounts

An important pattern: large, trusted Dutch accounts (broadcasters, political parties, news media) systematically use **no or barely any hashtags** in their videos. Examples from our corpus:

| Account type | Followers | % of videos with hashtags |
|---|---|---|
| Political party | 115K | 5% |
| Entertainment/humor platform | 832K | ~10% |
| Commercial broadcaster | 643K | ~15% |
| Public-service sports desk | 407K | ~20% |

These accounts rely on algorithmic reach, not hashtag discovery. For a researcher mapping filter bubbles these are exactly the most valuable data points — yet they are invisible in a hashtag-based graph.

**Implication for API design:** a `topics` or `content_categories` field per video (generated by TikTok itself for targeting) would close this gap. TikTok knows these categories internally — they are the basis of the recommendation system.

### Broken endpoint

- `/tiktok/tags/{tag}/videos` → consistently returns 404. We use `/tiktok/search/videos?query={tag}` as a workaround. It would be nice to have the tag endpoint fixed — the search endpoint sometimes returns less targeted results.

### The discovery problem: hashtag-based search results are not nationally filtered

The fundamental problem when building a national TikTok corpus: **the search API offers no language or country filter**. If you search `#grappig` or `#schoonmaken`, you get a mix of Dutch and international accounts that happen to use that hashtag. This forces us into an expensive post-hoc Dutch filter (fetch profile + fetch videos + language detection), whereas a `?language=nl` or `?country=NL` parameter would make this trivial.

**Quantitative effect:** without a language filter, ~50% of the accounts found are non-Dutch. That costs ~2 credits per non-Dutch account to reject (profile + first video page). With a country filter, scraping efficiency could drop from ~7 credits/Dutch account to ~3–4 — a doubling of the scraping yield.

### The mega-account problem: international creators with a Dutch audience

A related problem: large international accounts (skysports, CBS, ESPN) sometimes appear in search results on Dutch hashtags because they have a large Dutch viewer base. TikTok's own targeting knows this distinction — the difference between *creator nationality* and *audience nationality*. A `creator_country` field per account (separate from the audience) would solve this directly.

### 502 errors — also on the search endpoint, not only the video endpoints

On fast pagination the API inconsistently returns 502 errors. We built in a `random.sleep(0.5, 2.0)` as a workaround. But on 2026-06-12 we saw 502 errors also on `/tiktok/search/videos` — the search endpoint itself, under normal use and with ample sleep. This is different from the rate-limiting 502s on `/users/{username}/videos`. It looks like periodic server-side instability, independent of our request speed.

Effect: if the search endpoint fails mid-way through a chain-discovery probe, the session can hang (no response, no timeout message, just silence). We have a `timeout=20s` on requests, but on a 502 that returns a partial response this can take more than a minute before Python breaks the connection.

**Recommendation for the developer:** a `Retry-After` header on 502 responses would let clients wait intelligently instead of hanging. And/or an `X-RateLimit-Remaining` header so clients can adjust their speed before errors occur.

### Positive findings (what works well)

- Audio metadata (`audio_id`, `audio_title`) always available and reliable — valuable as an alternative co-occurrence signal alongside hashtags
- Caption/description (`content`) field: 95% populated, ~165 characters on average — usable for NLP analysis
- Pagination via `nextCursor` works stably
- Follower count at the profile level reliable enough for filtering

## What makes a bubble valid

1. **Size ≥ 8 accounts**
2. **Internal density ≥ 3%**
3. **Nameable** — one sentence about who these people are
4. **Distinctive hashtags** — ≥ 2–3 tags over-represented vs. the rest of the graph
5. **Describable boundary** — you can say what this bubble does NOT see
